Win the Day: Family Chores

A daily goals app for kids and parents. Kids tick off tasks on a chart in their favorite theme and earn streaks, badges and rewards. Parents set it up once, share chores between kids, and see everyone's progress at a glance.

What it does

✅ Daily charts

Each child gets their own list, grouped by morning, afternoon and evening, with pictures for kids still learning to read.

👥 Shared chores

Give a chore to one child, let kids take turns, or make it up for grabs for whoever gets there first.

🏆 Streaks, badges and rewards

Kids win the day, build streaks and earn the weekly or monthly reward you choose.

🪙 Points store

An optional store where kids spend points on prizes you set, with a way to turn points into allowance.

🖨️ Printable charts

Print a chart for the fridge, then scan it with your phone to update the app.

🔒 Parent controls

A parent PIN guards settings and past days. Parents and caregivers can join the same family.

Questions

Who is Win the Day for?

Families with kids of any age. Grown-ups can keep their own charts too, with themes made for adults.

Can the whole family use it on different phones?

Yes. Everyone joins the same family with a code, and check-offs sync between phones. Caregivers can be given access to just the kids they look after.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Check-offs are saved on the phone and sync when you are back online.

What happens to my family's information?

We collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell it, and you can delete your account and data at any time. See our privacy policy.

When can I get it?

Win the Day is in testing now and is coming soon to Google Play and the App Store. Write to us to hear when it launches.
