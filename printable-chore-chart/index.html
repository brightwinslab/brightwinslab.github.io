5 tips for a chore chart that sticks

  1. Start small. Three to five chores a day is plenty. Add more once the habit is there.
  2. Use pictures. Kids who can't read yet can follow a chart with pictures on their own.
  3. Tick it right away. Hang the chart where the chores happen and let kids mark each one as soon as it's done.
  4. Celebrate the day, not perfection. A finished day is a win, even if yesterday wasn't.
  5. Look back together. Spend five minutes at the end of the week counting wins and picking next week's reward.

Chore ideas by age

Ages 3 to 5: put toys away, put clothes in the hamper, help set the table, feed a pet with help, brush teeth.

Ages 6 to 8: make the bed, pack a school bag, clear the table, water plants, tidy their room, read for 15 minutes.

Ages 9 and up: homework, load or empty the dishwasher, take out the trash, fold laundry, walk the dog, practice an instrument.
